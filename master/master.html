MSc Project

Modelling and Control of a Twisted-String-Actuated Robotic Arm

Dynamic Modelling Using the Discrete-Time Transfer Matrix Method and PID-Based Motion Control

Fereshteh Khodaei  |  MSc Thesis, 2016  |  Shahrood University of Technology
Supervisors: Prof. M. Bamdad

Download Full Thesis Report Back to Home

Overview

This research focused on the dynamic modelling and control of a robotic arm actuated by a twisted-string mechanism. The project investigated the dynamic behaviour of the actuator and robotic system and developed a mathematical model suitable for analysing its motion and vibration characteristics.

Approach

    The robotic system was modelled using the Discrete-Time Transfer Matrix Method (DT-TMM), incorporating torsional and linear mass–spring elements to represent the dynamics of the twisted-string actuator. A PID controller was designed for motion control, and the system response was analysed under feedback control.

Result

The developed model was used to analyse the dynamic response, natural frequencies, and mode shapes of the twisted-string-actuated robotic system. The PID controller provided a framework for evaluating the motion-control behaviour of the robotic arm.

Outlook

The work provided a foundation for further research into flexible and cable-driven robotic systems, dynamic modelling, and advanced control strategies. The combination of dynamic modelling and feedback control is also relevant to the development of more adaptive and robust robotic systems.
