BSc Project

Modelling and Finite Element Analysis of the “CHEETA” Flexible Foot

CAD Modelling and Finite Element Analysis of a Cheetah-Inspired Prosthetic Foot

Fereshteh Khodaei  |  PhD Thesis, 2013  |  Babol Noshirvani University of Technology
Supervisors: Prof. M. Dardel

Download Full Thesis Report Back to Home

Overview

This project focused on the mechanical modelling and structural analysis of a flexible prosthetic foot inspired by the mechanical characteristics of a cheetah's leg. The aim was to develop a model and investigate its mechanical response under applied loading conditions.

Approach

    A CAD model of the prosthetic foot was developed, followed by finite element analysis to evaluate its structural behaviour. The analysis focused on parameters such as stress, strain, force, and reaction loads under the considered loading conditions.

Result

The finite element analysis provided an assessment of the mechanical response of the flexible foot and its structural behaviour under loading. The results were used to evaluate the design from a mechanical and structural perspective.

Outlook

The project provided early experience in biomechanical modelling, computational mechanics, and finite element analysis of prosthetic systems. This experience also established an early connection between mechanical engineering and healthcare-related technologies.
