PhD Project

Dynamic Mapping of Local Field Potential Signals across the Layers of the Primary Visual Cortex in Primate Monkeys

Computational Analysis of Neural Signal Dynamics, Layer-Specific Activity, and Information Transmission in Primate Visual Cortex

Fereshteh Khodaei  |  PhD Thesis, 2023  |  K. N. Toosi University of Technology
Supervisors: Prof. S. H. Sadati; Prof. R. Lashgari

Download Full Thesis Report Back to Home

Overview

This research investigated the spatial and temporal characteristics of Local Field Potential (LFP) signals recorded across different cortical layers and eccentricities of the primary visual cortex in primates. The study aimed to better understand how neural responses vary across cortical locations and how information related to visual stimuli is represented in neural signals.

Approach

    The research involved analysing experimentally recorded neural signals and investigating their morphology, polarity, response timing, and amplitude across different cortical locations. Computational approaches were used to examine stimulus-dependent neural activity and characterize layer-specific response patterns and information transmission.

Result

The analysis identified systematic variations in LFP response characteristics across cortical layers and eccentricities. The results provided insights into the spatial and temporal organization of neural activity in the primary visual cortex and contributed to understanding how visual information is represented in neural signals.

Outlook

The computational and signal-processing approaches developed in this research provide a foundation for further work involving neural interfaces, brain–machine interfaces, intelligent sensing, and data-driven modelling of complex biological systems. The experience also provides a basis for exploring the integration of neural signal processing with intelligent control and robotic technologies.
